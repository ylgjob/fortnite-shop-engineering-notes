Treat Fortnite Shop Active Time and Fetch Time as Separate Fields

November 26, 2026

A job may download Thursday’s shop at 00:12 even though the rotation began at midnight. A player reading the archive wants to know which rotation the offers belonged to. A maintainer looking at delays needs to know when they were fetched.

Store both timestamps. Use the active time to group offers into the correct rotation. Use the fetch time to explain how fresh the data is. Mark a missing active time as unknown or handle it using a documented rule.

The SkinRotation Fortnite shop history is a useful model for the date a reader expects to see. In a fixture, delay the fetch by twenty minutes and then replay it. The archive day should stay the same even if the new fetch time is later.
