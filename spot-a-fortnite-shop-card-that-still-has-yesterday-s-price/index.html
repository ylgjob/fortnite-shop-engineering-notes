Fortnite Shop Engineering Notes

Spot a Fortnite Shop Card That Still Has Yesterday’s Price

November 24, 2026

Pick one cosmetic and make two test rotations. Give the cosmetic the same ID and name in both. Change only the offer price, from 800 V-Bucks in the first rotation to 1,000 in the second. That makes a stale price easy to spot.

Load the first rotation, then switch to the second without reloading the whole browser tab. The title and picture should stay put while the price changes. Switch back to the first day and expect 800 again. Then open the second day in a new tab and compare the price shown there.

If the fresh tab is right and the old tab is wrong, look at the cache or the state stored for the card. The date and the offer need to travel together. The German Fortnite Item Shop on SkinRotation shows the kind of price label to watch during the test.
