Track Fortnite Shop Section Order Without Relying on the Labels

November 24, 2026

A section name can be translated or renamed. If the page sorts by the text in that label, the same offers can move just because someone changed a string. Readers who switch languages then have to find their way around again.

Keep a section ID beside its translated label. Use the supplied display order when the feed has one. Put a section without a known position somewhere predictable and keep its offers visible. Choose two sections from the German Fortnite Item Shop on SkinRotation to see how much room real labels take up.

For a simple test, give two sections the same label and leave a third label missing. Change the language and confirm that the order follows the IDs and recorded positions. Check where the unknown section ends up before deciding that it belongs at the top.
