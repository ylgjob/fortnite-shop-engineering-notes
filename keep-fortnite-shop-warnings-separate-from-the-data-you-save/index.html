Keep Fortnite Shop Warnings Separate From the Data You Save

November 24, 2026

A live shop page might say its latest refresh was late. If the whole response is saved to the archive, that warning can keep appearing weeks later. A reader might think something is broken today.

Save the rotation date and offers together. Record import warnings separately where maintainers can inspect them. When serving an old rotation, keep historical limits that still matter to the reader, such as a missing section. Leave a current retry message on the current page while it applies.

Open a past day from the SkinRotation Fortnite shop history to see what information belongs in a historical view. For a fixture, save one rotation and then simulate a failed refresh. Load the saved date through the archive route. You should find its list and date without picking up the warning from the new refresh.
