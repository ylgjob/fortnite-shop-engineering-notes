Keep a Change Log for Corrections to Fortnite Cosmetic Records

November 24, 2026

Someone finds a catalog entry in the wrong category and fixes it. A month later, another maintainer sees the old category in a snapshot and wonders whether there were two different records. A small change log can save a long hunt.

Keep the cosmetic ID fixed. Record the field, the old and new values, and the date of the correction. Add a short source note if an official catalog change explains the update. Only record the details needed to understand this correction.

Look through the SkinRotation cosmetic catalog to decide which fields matter to a player. Try correcting the category of one fictional outfit. Its catalog entry should move, the price recorded on an old shop day should stay put, and the log should contain one correction for that ID.
